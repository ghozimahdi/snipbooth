Email: **ghozi.dev@gmail.com** — we reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I retake one photo?**
After a booth session, tap the shot on the review screen and choose "Retake this shot". In the editor, select a photo and tap Photo booth.

**How do I print a 2×6 strip?**
Open a strip layout, tap Export, choose Print 2×6 and Save to Photos. The file is 600×1800 px at 300 dpi. Choose Print 4×6 to get two strips side by side on a standard 4×6 photo print; cut along the center line.

**Why is there a "Snipbooth" watermark?**
Free exports carry a small watermark. Snipbooth Pro removes it.

**How do I restore my subscription?**
Settings > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

**How do I cancel?**
iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Snipbooth. Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Snipbooth.

**Where are my photos stored?**
Only on your phone. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
