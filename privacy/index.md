# Snipbooth Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026

Snipbooth ("the app") is made by Ghozi Mahdi ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects. Short version: your photos stay on your phone, there is no account, and there are no ads or analytics.

## Photos and projects
Photos you take with the photo booth or import from your library are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us. A photo leaves your device only when you choose to save it to your photo library, share it, or print it.

The app imports photos through the system photo picker, so it can see only the photos you pick. It asks for camera access to take photos, and (on iOS) for permission to add photos to your library. It never asks to read your whole photo library and never uses the microphone.

## Purchases
Snipbooth Pro subscriptions are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the store. It does not receive your name or email from us. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Crash reports
If crash reporting is enabled in a build, the app sends crash diagnostics (stack traces, device model, OS version) through Sentry, without personal information, screenshots, or file paths. See https://sentry.io/privacy/.

## Seasonal catalog
The app may download a small public file with seasonal event dates from our website. The request contains no personal data.

## What we do not do
No advertising, no tracking across apps, no selling or sharing data for advertising, no account, no analytics SDK.

## Children
Snipbooth is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Deleting your data
Delete a strip in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data. Subscription records are kept by the store and RevenueCat as required for billing; contact us to request deletion of RevenueCat records.

## Changes
We will post changes on this page and update the effective date.
